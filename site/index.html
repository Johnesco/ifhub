IF Hub

Text adventures in the browser, with their source and walkthroughs beside them.

Every game runs in your browser through its engine's own web player, from Inform 7 and the original Infocom Z-machine to Ink, Rez and 1980s BASIC. Each game is its own site on GitHub Pages; the hub loads it in place and shows its source and walkthrough beside it. Pick a look to restyle the whole hub after a classic home computer.

Built on Inform 7, Parchment, Ink, Rez, Glulxe & Frotz, ZILF, Blorb and GitHub Pages, with Claude. Source.
